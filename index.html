Install in 30 seconds

No store wait · Direct install
01 Download & unzip

Click Download Plugin above and double-click cirkular-extension.zip to unzip the folder.

02 Enable Developer mode

Open chrome://extensions in your browser and switch on Developer mode in the top-right corner.

03 Drag & drop

Drag the unzipped cirkular-extension folder straight onto the extensions page. CIRKULAR opens automatically.
